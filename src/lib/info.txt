This folder contains shared low-level utilities used across the application.

What goes here:
- prisma.ts: the central database connection.
- auth helpers: reusable authentication helpers.
- permission helpers: reusable role and ownership checks.
- formatters: shared date, currency or text formatting helpers.
- small utilities used by multiple features.

Important rule:
Business logic such as creating clients, booking appointments or editing plans should not go here. That belongs inside src/features.
