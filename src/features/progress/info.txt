This feature manages client progress tracking.

Subfolders:
- components/: progress charts, progress tables, log forms and exercise progress views.
- server/: queries and actions for reading progress data and creating progress logs.
- schemas/: validation schemas for progress log forms.
- types/: TypeScript types related to progress logs and chart data.
- utils/: progress-specific helpers, such as preparing chart data or calculating progress changes.

This feature should contain everything related to ProgressLog and progress per exercise.
