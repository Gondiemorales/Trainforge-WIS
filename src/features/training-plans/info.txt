This feature manages personalised training plans.

Subfolders:
- components/: plan builders, plan cards, assigned exercise lists and plan detail UI.
- server/: queries and actions for creating, updating and archiving training plans.
- schemas/: validation schemas for training plans and plan exercises.
- types/: TypeScript types related to plans, plan exercises and form data.
- utils/: plan-specific helpers, such as formatting sets, reps or training days.

This feature should contain everything related to TrainingPlan and PlanExercise.
