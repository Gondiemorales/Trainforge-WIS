This feature manages the exercise catalogue.

Subfolders:
- components/: exercise tables, exercise forms, exercise cards and exercise filters.
- server/: queries and actions for creating, editing, listing and archiving exercises.
- schemas/: validation schemas for exercise forms.
- types/: TypeScript types related to exercises.
- utils/: exercise-specific helpers, such as formatting muscle groups or difficulty levels.

This feature should contain everything related to the ExerciseDictionary model.
