This feature manages appointments and calendar scheduling.

Subfolders:
- components/: calendar views, appointment cards, appointment forms and scheduling UI.
- server/: queries and actions for creating, updating, cancelling and listing appointments.
- schemas/: validation schemas for appointment forms.
- types/: TypeScript types related to appointments and calendar events.
- utils/: appointment-specific helpers, such as formatting dates, times and statuses.

This feature should contain everything related to booking and displaying client appointments.
