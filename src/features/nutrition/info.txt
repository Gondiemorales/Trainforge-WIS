This feature manages AI-assisted nutrition plans.

Subfolders:
- components/: nutrition forms, meal plan cards, editable meal plan views and published plan UI.
- server/: queries and actions for generating, reviewing, publishing and reading nutrition plans.
- schemas/: validation schemas for nutrition inputs, meal plans and AI-generated data.
- types/: TypeScript types related to nutrition plans, meals, macros and AI responses.

This feature should contain everything related to NutritionPlan and reviewed AI-assisted meal plans.
