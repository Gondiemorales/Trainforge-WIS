This feature manages personal trainer clients.

Subfolders:
- components/: client tables, client cards, client forms and client detail UI.
- server/: queries and actions for creating, reading, updating and deleting clients.
- schemas/: validation schemas for client forms.
- types/: TypeScript types related to clients and client form data.
- utils/: client-specific helpers, such as formatting goals or experience levels.

This feature should contain everything related to trainer-managed client profiles.
